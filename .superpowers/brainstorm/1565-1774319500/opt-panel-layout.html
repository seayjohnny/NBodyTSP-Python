Optimization Panel Layout

Where should the optimization UI appear during a run?

A

Overlay Panel (like JS version)

Fixed panel centered at top of screen, overlaying the canvas. Shows trial progress, convergence chart, current/best params. Has a "Stop & Use Best" button. Disappears when optimization ends.

Replace Control Panel

The right-side control panel transforms into an optimization panel during the run. Sliders are replaced with progress display, convergence chart, and parameter selection checkboxes. Reverts to normal controls when done.
